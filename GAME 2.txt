🕹️ How the Game Works
Home Screen → Choose Track → Play → Collect 3 Flags → Win or Crash → Repeat
Flow:

Home Screen — shows the "MOTO Trials" logo, PLAY button, Skins button, and your total ⭐ count
Track Sheet — opens an overlay with all 10 levels in a 2-column grid. Locked levels show 🔒
Game — you ride a motorcycle through a procedurally shaped terrain
3 Checkpoint Flags sit at 33%, 66%, and 100% of the track. Each flag collected = 1 ⭐
Reach the 3rd flag = Level Complete 🏆. Your rider's head hits the ground = GAME OVER 💥
Stars unlock the next level immediately (even mid-run)
Garage — spend collected stars to unlock new bike skins
