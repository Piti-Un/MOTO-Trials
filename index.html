The Game
MOTO
Trials
by Antigravity Games · 2026
MOTO Trials is a mobile-first physics motorcycle game where you ride through 10 increasingly brutal tracks, collect checkpoint flags to earn stars, and unlock new levels and premium bike skins along the way.
🏍️ Racing ⚙️ Physics 📱 Mobile 🏆 Progression 🎨 Customization
How It Works

Simple Controls. Deep Challenge.

01
▶
Gas (Right)
Hold the right button or press → to accelerate. The throttle builds progressively — slow start, fast finish.
02
◀
Brake (Left)
Hold the left button or press ← to brake, lean back mid-air, or slow down on steep descents.
03
🏁
Collect Flags
Three checkered flags sit at 33%, 66%, and 100% of each track. Each one earns you a ⭐ star.
04
💥
Don't Crash!
If your rider's head touches the ground, it's Game Over. You'll have a short crash countdown before losing.
05
🔓
Unlock Levels
Earning your first star on any level automatically unlocks the next one in the sequence.
06
🎨
Unlock Skins
Total stars across all levels unlock premium bike skins in the Garage — from Neon to BMW S1000RR.
